# Allemande

[Allemande](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Allemande) from [Lute Suite in E minor, BWV 996](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lute_Suite_in_E_minor,_BWV_996) by [Johann Sebastian Bach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johann_Sebastian_Bach).

This is not how the piece is usually played, and probably not how it was intended to be played. The allemande is traditionally a rather serious and measured dance, with little or no syncopation.

All the notes are here (except for one, which is too difficult for me to play), but I've added plenty of slides and slurs, often emphasizing offbeats rather than strong beats. It's a little experiment with rhythm and phrasing, perhaps not entirely in the spirit of a traditional allemande.

If you like it, enjoy it!
